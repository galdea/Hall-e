Your invitation

Checking the invitation…

Invitations work once and expire after 10 minutes. Only connect a device you trust. Hall-e will ask you to confirm in the app.

If Hall-e does not open, open Hall-e on Android and choose Shared sessions, or open Hall-e on Mac and choose Devices & shared meetings in Settings. Paste the copied invitation. Do not share this invitation in a public place.

What pairing shares

Paired devices can share meeting titles, project names, optional meeting links, times, and recording-segment status. These meeting details are encrypted between paired devices. The coordinator can see device names and IDs, meeting/session IDs, recording lease state, and segment device/timing/status metadata. It never receives audio.

Recording starts only after someone acts in Hall-e on the device that will record, grants microphone access, and confirms the recording notice. Audio stays on that device.

If Hall-e does not open, open Shared sessions on Android or Devices & shared meetings in Mac Settings, then paste the invitation.
