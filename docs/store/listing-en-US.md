# Hall-e — App Store listing draft (English, United States)

Draft only. Confirm the final feature set and sandbox behavior on an installed App Store package before publishing these claims. This listing is for the Mac App Store edition: recordings capture microphone audio only, from the input you choose.

## App information

- **Name:** Hall-e
- **Subtitle:** Meetings, notes, reminders
- **Promotional text:** Keep your meeting day, recordings, and project notes in one calm workspace.
- **Description:**

  Hall-e helps you prepare for meetings and keep the work that follows easy to find.

  **See what’s next**  
  Connect a calendar available on your Mac, choose which calendars to show, and review your upcoming meetings and reminders.

  **Record microphone audio**  
  Start and stop a recording with the microphone input you choose. This Mac App Store edition captures microphone audio only; it does not capture audio from other apps. Use supported on-device Apple Speech transcription, or connect your own optional cloud transcription account and explicitly allow audio processing. Read, search, and export transcripts; cloud transcription can add anonymous speaker labels.

  **Keep notes and follow-ups together**  
  Write notes alongside a meeting, organize meetings into projects, and find project follow-ups grounded in your notes. Export notes and transcripts as Markdown.

  Hall-e does not include shared transcription credits or require a Hall-e account. Optional cloud processing requires your own provider account, internet access, and consent. Calendar access, microphone access, and notifications are requested only for the features you choose. Tell meeting participants when you record, and follow the consent rules that apply to you.

  Requires macOS 14 or later. On-device transcription depends on macOS language and model availability. Calendar availability depends on accounts configured in macOS.
