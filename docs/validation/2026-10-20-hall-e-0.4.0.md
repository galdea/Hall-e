## Changes

- Configurable meeting reminder lead time in setup and settings, bounded catch-up and snooze handling, permission diagnostics, and once-per-morning mouse-activity agenda on macOS.
- Today shows the current/next meeting and separates earlier meetings. Project follow-ups replace the standalone Actions navigation, with durable completion state and source-note links.
- Guided ambiguous-transcription recovery validates the existing Speechmatics job before retrieval. Creating a separate paid attempt requires explicit confirmation; the original transcript remains available.
- Mac App Store compilation disables incompatible direct-edition integrations and uses security-scoped notes-folder bookmarks. Signing and sandbox installation remain gated.
- A native Android companion reads selected device calendars, schedules local reminders, and stores project notes locally. Its morning summary runs on first app opening between 04:00 and noon. It has no audio recording or Mac synchronization.
