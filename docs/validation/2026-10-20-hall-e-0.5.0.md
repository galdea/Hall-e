# Hall-e 0.5.0 / Android 0.2.0 candidate validation

Final macOS source: `f5dde2f0c22be6277c9d19407134b415897c68c9`.
macOS candidate: **0.5.0, build 2026102010**. Android: **0.2.0, version code 2**.
These are local/store-test candidates, not public releases. The
[0.4.0 validation record](2026-10-20-hall-e-0.4.0.md) retains the earlier
reminder, workspace, signed sandbox and notification checks.

## Changes

- Configurable reminders, visible delivery diagnostics, a responsive morning
  workspace, guided transcription recovery, and clearer Today/Projects views.
- Android quick meeting creation with date/time, an existing or new project,
  and explicit phone microphone recording, stop, playback and sharing.
- Optional device pairing and shared meetings across networks. Meeting titles,
  projects, links and scheduled times are encrypted on device. A short-lived
  exclusive lease coordinates recording and handoff; each device still needs
  its own user action and microphone permission. Audio stays on its recorder.
  This is not full calendar, notes, project-library or audio synchronization.
- Shared-session and startup credit-check Keychain reads are asynchronous;
  denied shared credentials fail closed without treating the device as unpaired.
- The unused OpenClaw/Gemini structured-report integration is retired, including
  its settings, client, pipeline and automatic retries. Historical files and
  serialized metadata remain compatible. Separate configured AI providers retain
  their own controls. Historical backfill persists and verifies its no-text-AI
  policy before queueing, including recovery after restart; audio consent,
  manifest approval, sample acceptance and spend checks remain required.

## Remaining release gates

- Actual Mac/Pixel pairing and recording handoff are **unverified**. An expired
  invitation was rejected correctly; a fresh QR could not be scanned during
  this session. A later Mac microphone attempt remained pending permission and
  was cancelled by quitting. No successful new shared Mac capture is claimed.
- The final sandbox candidate still requires installed shared-device/Keychain
  UI QA. Earlier signed sandbox checks covered calendar access, six-second
  microphone capture/playback, notes-folder access after moving/relaunching,
  notification-delivery feedback and localhost OAuth callback handling. They
  do not establish current shared-device acceptance. Full Google browser OAuth,
  external-volume and revoked-folder-permission scenarios remain untested.
- App Store Connect has a saved **0.5.0 listing draft**. No build or app
  screenshots have been uploaded. `apple-0.5-draft.png` is local evidence of the
  listing, not an uploaded store asset. Exact-candidate App Privacy answers,
  review metadata, final screenshots, required release checks and submission
  remain pending. Transporter installation was blocked by automatic approval
  review, which did not recognize the supplied authorization; no workaround
  was used.
- Google Play accepted and processed the **0.2.0/code 2 AAB as internal draft
  release 2**. It has not been published. The two warnings concern one TV losing
  compatibility and absence of a mapping file (minification is disabled).
  Preserve the public-access closed-test requirement of 12 testers for 14 days.
- No hosted GitHub Actions were dispatched. Required release/security gates
  remain intact; local evidence does not replace hosted release validation.
